Mental Health in Australia

draft

Mental health affects millions of Australians. This page looks at how common mental disorders and psychological distress are, which age groups and sexes are most affected, how things have changed over time, and who is getting help.

1. How common is it?

43% of Australians aged 16–85 have had a mental disorder in their life; 21.5% in the last 12 months.

Reported mental/behavioural conditions have risen from 9.6% (2001) to 26.1% (2022).

6. Conclusion

key takeaways:

Mental ill-health is common in Australia. About 1 in 5 people aged 16–85 had a mental disorder in the past year, and young people are the most affected. Almost half of women aged 16–24 had a disorder in the last 12 months.
